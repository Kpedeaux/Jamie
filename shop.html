Seasons in Ritual

Things you can touch and feel

Independence St. is the talking side of what I do. Seasons in Ritual is the hands-on side: oils, burns, and planners made in small batches with love, Reiki, and intention.

Ritual oils & essences

Every batch works with the sky. Dark moon oils are cleansed in the void, open and receiving. Full moon oils are filled under her light, ready to initiate something new.

The base is avocado or olive oil with botanicals Jamie grows or gathers, infused outdoors over about six weeks. Body oils come in rose, jasmine, and gardenia. Anointing oils dress your candles for ritual work.

From $15. Best used within six months, and Jamie will tell you which is which.

Zodiac burns

A loose botanical blend for each zodiacal season, grown and gathered from Jamie's yard and her friends' gardens, packed in compostable tea-bag pouches.

Read the intention card out loud, then burn the blend on a charcoal disc, dress a candle with it, or just crush it in your hands and hold it to your heart. They're made to break things up, move things through, clear things out, and call things in.

Fair warning from Jamie: some of them are just going to smell like burning leaves.

The seasonal planner

A three-month digital calendar built on the witch's wheel: moon cycles, moon names, esoteric dates, journal prompts set by a seasonal card pull, and a few spells. Solstice to equinox, four times a year.
