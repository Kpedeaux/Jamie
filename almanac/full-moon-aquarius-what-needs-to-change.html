The Almanac · July 23, 2026

The Aquarius full moon is showing you where you stopped asking questions

The moon goes full at 6 degrees of Aquarius on July 29, sitting with Pluto and Juno, staring straight across at the sun and Jupiter in Leo.

Hey friends. The full moon lands on July 29 at 6 degrees of Aquarius, with the sun sitting at 6 degrees of Leo directly across from her. I am writing this on the 23rd, which is early, and I am doing that on purpose. There is a lot of energy moving right now and I would rather you have a week with it than an afternoon.

When the moon is full, she is directly opposite the sun and reflecting all of him back to us. That is when we are at our most psychic. It is also the moment in the cycle when the big picture gets lit up. Full moons are not here to hand you a to-do list. They are here to show you the thing you have not been looking at.

Here is what makes this one different. The moon is conjunct Pluto and Juno, and the sun is in an exact conjunction with Jupiter. Juno rules the calendar, she rules time, and Jupiter is her consort. So here they are, on opposite ends of the wheel, pulling everything toward balance. That means the conversation is about relationship. Not only who you are with. How you are in relationship with yourself.

Mercury stations direct on the 23rd, thank you Jesus. We are still in the shadow period, but we are out of the worst of it, and you should start to feel your words come back.

The other piece is Mars at 21 degrees of Gemini squaring Venus at 21 degrees of Virgo. Mars is that divine masculine energy inside all of us, the part that moves, and in Gemini he wants to think his way there. Venus in Virgo wants things resolved. Put those two in a square and you get a very specific question: how do you talk to yourself in order to get yourself moving? Kindly? Or the other way?

Notice where you are talking to yourself in fear. That is the past. Virgo is the present.

Pay attention to what you are listening to, too. What you put on to motivate yourself. What you reach for when you are trying to resolve something. What you play in the background while you are getting your point across to a person who is not even in the room.

Aquarius is fixed air and Leo is fixed fire, and both of them are sitting at these very early degrees. Six degrees is page energy. Unjaded, optimistic, still convinced it can be done. And a full moon in Aquarius right at the front end of the age of Aquarius feels like a christening to me. We are going to have a lot of these over the next 2,500 years. This is one of the first ones.

What Aquarius is good at is finding the weak point. The thing that stopped making sense a long time ago and nobody said anything about it. That is the flavor of this moon. You are going to look around and go, wait, why have we been doing it like that? It does not have to be mean or harsh. It is a conversation. And some of it is going to be about critical thinking, about the joy of figuring something out for yourself, and where we have quietly let that muscle go slack.

People are talking about the Pluto and Jupiter opposition as the part where things happen fast. I do read it that way, but not in the way it sounds. It is not men with bayonets marching down the street. It is a revolution inside your own chest. If you have been struggling with something and it suddenly makes sense, and you go, oh my word, I think that is it, that changes everything.

Aquarius is the water bearer. She brings in everything from the cosmos, and she understands it has to be contained. That is the boundaries piece. We are coming out of an age where everyone was in everyone else's business and every emotion belonged to all of us. These fixed signs want structure. They want it to make sense.

So here is what I would do with it. Start paying attention on the 26th. The 29th is the height. The 30th and 31st and the 1st are when it starts to wane back out. Watch where your thoughts change. Watch where your heart changes. Watch for the fear talk, the if we do not do this then everyone dies talk, because it is at a fever pitch right now and it is not information, it is just fear.

And know that this one is not one and done. The moon keeps moving, but Pluto and Jupiter stay inside each other's range for a while. Let that opposition do its work. Let things transmute. Let things die, because that is what makes room, and the void is where creation actually happens.

If you have questions, reach out. I am glad we are here doing this together.
