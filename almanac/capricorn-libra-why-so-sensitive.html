Capricorns and Libras: here is why you have been so sensitive

Four clients in a row, then the phone calls, then the texts. Every single one a Capricorn or a Libra. So I pulled cards.

Hey friends, it is Jamie. Last week I was working with clients and I was about four in when I realized that every single person I had seen for two days was either a Capricorn or a Libra. Then a couple of friends called and texted the same day. Same two signs. Then Monday, two more Capricorns. That has never happened to me before, so I am going to talk about it, and I pulled cards.

Capricorn and Libra are both cardinal signs. The other two are Aries and Cancer, and I have a theory about why I have not heard from those. Aries has Saturn and Neptune sitting right there and has been dealing with a huge amount of change already. Cancer has Jupiter, which is a big energy in its own right. So the Aries and Cancer people are in it, but it is a different kind of in it.

Here is what I think is happening. Uranus has just moved into Gemini at 1 degree, and it is going to be there for the next seven or eight years. The moon had already moved into Gemini, the sun was about to. And the peak of all these calls was at the dark moon in Taurus, which is the void, at the very end of Taurus season.

Capricorn, you initiate how we work. Your ruler is Saturn, and Saturn is in Aries. Aries is the I am presence, and it is showing up right now with more emotional intelligence than it used to have. What I kept hearing from you all is that you feel sensitive, sometimes like you are being personally attacked, and you are not used to it. Not because you are soulless sea goats. Because you are used to a certain pace and a certain way of getting through.

Almost every one of you already knew you were about to move into a new phase. Changing jobs, expanding the business, changing a relationship status. And you had been working on it for years, so it was not a whim, and part of you knew it was fine. But something else was coming in and it was making you tender.

Capricorn, you are always going to be the goat. But you are moving into the sea part now, and the sea is feelings.

In the age we are leaving, you could grit your teeth and get on with it. That is over. The way you communicate now is energetic, and it is going to keep building. Your opposite sign is Cancer, cardinal water, so of course the feelings are showing up. Earth has to have water. And Saturn, your ruler, wants boundaries, so this is about setting new ones. Boundaries that match how you feel and let you be proud of what you are working on. If it does not feel good and whole, it does not get done well anymore.

I will say one practical thing here, because I mean it. If you are in a heavy emotional state for more than about six weeks, reach out to a therapist. If you want help sooner than that, get it sooner. There is no rule that says you have to wait.

Your cards, Capricorn, came from the Wild Unknown Alchemy deck: Tin, and The Void.

Tin was known in medieval times as the grey wolf of alchemy, because of how unpredictable it was and how it would eat away at materials in the laboratory. Gold is one of the few things that withstands it. So the card has to be read gently. On one hand it is the wild free spirit that wants expression. On the other it is a fierceness that can turn corrosive fast. The message is to channel that toward creativity. Take the project or the adventure that is calling you instead of focusing on why you cannot. The grey wolf responds to respect, not repression. Which I love for you, because you are known to just work, work, work.

And The Void, on the dark moon, for a group of earth signs. It says it cannot be understood by alchemy or chemistry or science or spirituality. It shows up when something has been released, a relationship, a title, a way of life, a way you thought about yourself. Meditation is critical right now. Just sit and breathe. And if the word meditation makes you itch, then call it breathing. Notice the air going in through your nose and out through your mouth. That is enough to reset.

Now my lovable Libras. Cardinal air, and your opposite sign is Aries, where absolutely everything is happening at the moment. You have also been feeling raw, and I think it is because you are natural helpers, and the place that turns toxic for you is people pleasing. So when people say things that are not pleasing, especially near you, you take it personally.

Here is what I want you to hear. If it is not your life, it is not about you. I had a client come in upset about things people in her friend group were bringing up, things she could get pulled into, but which were not hers. A friend of mine is an interior designer, and someone made a nasty comment implying his work was cutting into how they made money. That is not true and it was never about him. That person is frustrated about their own situation. It is not because he painted a wall blue.

Your cards were Magnum Opus and the Alchemical Wedding, which look almost like a matched pair, which is very on brand for the scales.

Magnum Opus is attention, devotion, and vocation. The great work shimmers in the distance and we keep asking when we will get there and when the effort will be recognized. The card says the alchemical garden grows from seeds tended by devoted hands. You cannot force the great work or claim it or complete it. What it says is: you are in the right place, the most important place for you. Tend the youngest growth. Be delicate and precise.

The Alchemical Wedding is celebration. What was once lost has been found. When it arrives it is important to mark the occasion and to honor the people who helped you get here. Energy radiates from the uniting of opposites and the redrawing of the blueprint. The new form has arrived.

So if you are feeling tender, Libra, it is because you are exactly where you have been working to get to, and arriving is its own adjustment. It is like graduating. You finally figured out how to do the last thing, and now there is a new thing. Good job. I am proud of you. Get yourself back in balance and remember to ask the question that always works for you: what in this situation makes me feel out of balance?
