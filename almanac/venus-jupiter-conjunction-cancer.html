The Almanac · June 9, 2026

Venus and Jupiter meet in Cancer, and they only do this every twelve years

People are calling it the luckiest day of the year. Any day can be a lucky day. Here is what I am actually reading in it.

Hello everyone, it is Jamie. Venus and Jupiter are both at 25 degrees of Cancer, in an exact conjunction. They meet in some sign about once a year. They meet in Cancer once every twelve years.

There is a lot going around about this being the luckiest day of the year and the luckiest day of the next twelve. Honestly, any day can be a lucky day. Let us not limit ourselves in a moment that is about abundance.

First, the practical part. I read conjunctions with about a ten degree orb, some people use seven. So even as Venus moves on to 26 and 27 and into Leo, they are still in each other's energy, which means you can keep working with this until around the 17th or the 19th depending on which orb you like.

Cancer is cardinal water, and cardinal signs initiate. They are the first feelings we have about something. The way Aries is the moment you realize you are a self, cardinal water is the first time you realize love exists and what it does to you. Cancer rules the home, and I mean that in every layer of it. Where you live. Your body, which is the original home. Your community, which is also a home.

Venus is the divine feminine, which is how we receive. She is love, abundance, money, and self-worth. Jupiter is luck and expansion, he rules Sagittarius, and at his highest that sign is live and let live. That works for you, great, it hurts nobody, even better, keep going.

When we are firing on all cylinders we are receiving and moving, receiving and moving. It gets choppy the moment we try to split those apart and assign them.

The masculine and feminine energies are in all of us, whatever your gender, whatever you present as. Feminine is how we receive and allow. Masculine is how we move it through. That the two look a certain way in the world is a human construct. The energies themselves are just receiving and moving.

So what should you do with a conjunction like this. Check in with where you actually are. Then go back and check in with where you were twelve years ago, because that is the last time these two sat down together in this sign, and they have twelve years of material to compare.

The moon is helping. She is in Aries, cardinal fire, the I am presence, conjunct Neptune, which brings the unconscious to the conscious, and conjunct Vesta, the light of the hearth, and Saturn, which is stability. In Moon School we pulled the take action card, and I said I could not believe it, but I absolutely could, because that is how spirit works.

The maturity in taking action here is that action includes allowing. It is reading the room. It is watching what the universe is bringing in that you can actually see, and then moving on that. It is not having your hands in everything at once. Rest is part of it. Taking a time out so the universe has room to put things together before you show up and step into them.

One thing I have never liked about astrology is the framing that you missed it. The day passed, too bad, catch it in twelve years. That has always bothered me, because energy is eternal. If you find this a week from now, or a year from now, you can tap into it. That is how energy works. The only thing that removes energy is transmutation.

So ask. Ask for more luck, more money, more love, more joy, more security, more safety. Ask for things to be more affordable. Whether you mean your body home, your actual home, or your community home, let Jupiter expand on it.
